The Equitable Corporation

Equitable Corporation

Type: Corporate structure

Standard: Equitable Standard

Founder: Ethan Wei, MD

What it is

An Equitable Corporation is a company built on a simple belief: the people who create the value should keep it. Much of today's tech is built on extraction — your data, attention, and effort are used to generate value that flows away from you. The Equitable Corporation inverts that: it is structured so the value created on the platform is not extracted, and the core promises that protect users are locked so they cannot be quietly revised away.

The core commitments

Every Equitable Corporation commits to the same locked set of principles. In short: user data stays owned by the user and is never sold, mined, or read; the company cannot be acquired by private equity or large firms; and the rules themselves are locked so they cannot be quietly revised away. Transparency and auditability are built in, so the commitments can be verified rather than merely trusted.

Why a corporation, not a non-profit

The structure needs real upkeep, legal standing, and earnings to stay afloat against other corporations. A non-profit would cap the founder's earning potential and can't compete the way a for-profit can. The Equitable Corporation lets a company sustain itself and compete — while its core commitments remain locked and irreversible.
